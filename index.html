Welcome!

I am an Economist at the Central Bank of the Republic of Turkey, working in the Structural Economic Research Department, within the Employment and Household Behavior Research Group. I hold a PhD in Economics from Boğaziçi University, with a specialization in Applied Microeconomics. My research interests lie in Labor and Demographic Economics, with a particular focus on Migration. I am especially interested in using causal methods to generate insights that support evidence-based policy-making.
